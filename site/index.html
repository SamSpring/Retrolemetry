What’s on screen

Your Mac. And a little more.

System readings, the forecast and a view of the wider world. Here’s what you can keep an eye on.

01 / Your system

  • CPU & GPUUsage and history graphs, plus system load averages.
  • RAM & diskUsage percentages and actual used/free capacity.
  • NetworkUpload and download rates with live traffic history.
  • UptimeDays, hours and minutes since your Mac last started.
  • Battery readingsOptional battery power flow and voltage.

GPU and battery readings depend on hardware support. Battery flow is not total Mac power use.

02 / Your weather

  • Current conditionsTemperature, feels-like temperature and conditions.
  • The detailsHumidity, wind speed and rainfall.
  • Hourly forecastThe next 12 hours of temperature and precipitation.
  • Daily outlookA configurable 3–7-day forecast for the week ahead.
  • Regional scansForecast maps for Israel, North America and Europe.

Regional scans use weather-model forecasts, rather than live precipitation radar.

03 / Markets & time

  • World clocksThree configurable locations, with automatic daylight-saving changes.
  • Stock watchlistsYour choice of symbols, with prices and daily changes.
  • Selectable chartsSwitch between Today, 1 Week, 1 Month and 1 Year.
  • Currency historyUSD→ILS daily reference rates and a history chart.
  • A world in motionA rotating globe with clock-based market-session lights.

Live stock quotes need your own Finnhub API key. Currency rates are daily reference data.

Plus Signal Analysis. A live vector grid, spinning signal or GPU history, with CPU, memory and network traces.

Modular by design

Make space for what matters.

Each view is made of modules you can move, resize or hide. Keep the readings you want, then make the console feel like yours.

Your layout

Arrange modules within each view, adjust text sizes and hide the things you don’t need. Fine-tune individual views or share typography settings across them.

Your atmosphere

Choose phosphor green or synthwave. Dial in CRT curvature, bloom, scan lines, noise and vignette. Reorder the four views, set automatic cycling and choose a transition style.

Your second screen

Made for smaller displays, including iPad with Sidecar. Match your display, use a preset or set custom dimensions. It remembers its screen, returns after reconnection and stays hidden when that screen is away.

Weather uses Open-Meteo with approximate or fixed public coordinates. Optional market quotes use your own Finnhub key stored in macOS Keychain. No accounts, ads, analytics or bundled credentials.
